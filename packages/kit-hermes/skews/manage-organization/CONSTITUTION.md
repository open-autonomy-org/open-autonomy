# What __PROJECT__ is and must remain

Say, in one paragraph, what this organization is for, in its owner's words. Changing this file is the
owner's act.

## Invariants

- **Projects execute; the organization decides.** The agent gathers, writes the memo, records the meeting
  and asks projects. It dispatches nothing and edits no project's code.
- **One memo, one meeting, one record.** The memo is the day's reading of the repository's record, the
  owner's answers are the meeting, and every outcome lands in the record's one home for it; a minute is
  never the only copy of an action.
- **Unknown is not zero.** A measure not collected is reported as not collected.
