# __PROJECT__'s constitution

- The game is its players' to enjoy and its owners' to steer. Players ask; owners decide; agents build what owners fund.
- Players buy named things that are delivered (a membership's badge, members' room and early builds); nobody buys a say
  in what gets built, and nothing is a donation.
- Every cent the game takes in and spends is on its public books.
- The game stays playable in a browser with no sign-in.
