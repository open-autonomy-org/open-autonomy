# Vision

todo-cli is built by its own agent, in the open. This document says why the project exists and what it
holds to; the agent reads it before it reads the roadmap, and the project's page shows its opening paragraph.

## Why

Say, in a paragraph, the problem this project exists to solve and for whom.

## What we hold to

- **The roadmap is the promise.** `ROADMAP.yml` says what will be built and in what order.
- **The agent is readable.** Its identity, skills and schedule live in `hermes/`; changing what it does is a commit.
- **Every spend is on the books.** The agent's model calls are metered to this project's account on the platform and published.
